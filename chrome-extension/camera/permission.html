Gaze Scroll

Let SETU use your camera, once

Gaze Scroll moves the page as you move your head, so you can read without a mouse or a keyboard. To do that it needs to see you.

Granting it here grants it to SETU itself, so it works on every site afterwards and you are never asked again. Granting it on an individual website only ever worked on that one website — which is why it kept being blocked.

  • Video is measured in the browser and thrown away, frame by frame.
  • No image, video, or recording is stored, uploaded, or sent anywhere.
  • The camera only runs while Gaze Scroll is switched on.
  • You can withdraw this at any time from the camera icon in the address bar.
