      Privacy & Safety

      What leaves this browser

      • Reading tools Bionic text, line focus, reading ruler, themes, auto-scroll and browser speech run entirely on your local device. Zero bytes uploaded.
      • AI features When you ask Commander a question, request a 3-step path, or explain a chart, the visible text of the active page (and for charts, an element crop) is sent to your configured engine endpoint.
      • Gaze tracking Camera frames are analyzed on an in-memory canvas and immediately discarded. No video is ever recorded, stored, or transmitted.
      • Your details Stored in this browser's local storage on this device only — deliberately not synced to your Google account. Filling a form sends nothing at all. When the Copilot plans a task it tells the engine which details you have saved and never what they are; the real values go into the page here. ID and bank details are never typed without you confirming that exact step.
      • Zero password exposure Password fields and sensitive auth tokens are strictly sanitized and never included in page context sent to AI models.
