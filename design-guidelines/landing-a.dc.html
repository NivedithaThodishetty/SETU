The problem

Density is a disability barrier

A benefits portal, a tenancy agreement, a lecture handout. The information is there. The shape of it is what keeps people out.

The move

Nothing is thrown away. The same words, re-laid so your eye can hold a line and your attention can hold a branch.

The promise

It stays yours

Maps are saved in your browser. No account, no upload, no model trained on the thing you were struggling to read.
